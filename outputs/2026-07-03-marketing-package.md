**Recipe: Roshini's Nutrimix No-Bake Energy Bites**

These quick, delicious, and nutrient-packed energy bites are perfect for lunchboxes, after-school snacks, or even a healthy treat for parents! They're customizable and incredibly easy to make.

*   **Yields:** 12-15 bites
*   **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
*   **Cook Time:** 0 minutes (Chill time: 30 minutes)

**Ingredients:**

*   1 cup Roshini's Nutrimix
*   1/2 cup pitted dates (Medjool or any soft variety), finely chopped or pureed
*   2 tablespoons nut butter (almond, peanut, or cashew butter)
*   1-2 tablespoons ghee or coconut oil (melted, if solid)
*   1/4 teaspoon cardamom powder (optional, for flavor)
*   2-3 tablespoons warm water (only if needed to bind)
*   1/4 cup shredded coconut or finely chopped nuts (for rolling, optional)

**Instructions:**

1.  **Prepare Dates:** If your dates are firm, soak them in warm water for 5-10 minutes, then drain well. Alternatively, you can briefly warm them in a microwave with a tablespoon of water to soften, then mash or puree them into a paste.
2.  **Combine Ingredients:** In a medium-sized mixing bowl, combine Roshini's Nutrimix, the mashed/pureed dates, nut butter, and melted ghee/coconut oil. Add cardamom powder if using.
3.  **Mix Thoroughly:** Use your hands or a sturdy spoon to mix all the ingredients until a thick, uniform dough forms. It should be sticky enough to hold together when pressed. If the mixture feels too dry, add warm water one tablespoon at a time until it reaches the right consistency.
4.  **Roll the Bites:** Take small portions of the mixture (about 1 tablespoon each) and roll them into compact, bite-sized balls.
5.  **Coat (Optional):** If desired, roll the formed energy bites in shredded coconut or finely chopped nuts for an extra layer of texture and flavor.
6.  **Chill:** Place the finished energy bites on a plate or in an airtight container and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to help them firm up.
7.  **Serve & Store:** Enjoy these healthy bites chilled. Store any leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to a week.

**Tip:** For extra crunch, you can add a tablespoon of finely chopped almonds or cashews directly into the mixture!
