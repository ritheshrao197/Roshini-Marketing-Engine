<li>Snacks: Mix with yogurt and a sprinkle of chia seeds for a protein‑rich parfait.</li>

<li>Baking: Substitute up to 30 % of refined flour with Nutrimix in muffins, pancakes, or bread for added moisture and nutrients.</li>

<h2>FAQs</h2>
<p><strong>Q: Are sprouted millets safe for children?</strong>
A: Yes. The sprouting process reduces anti‑nutrients and enhances digestibility, making Nutrimix suitable for toddlers over one year when introduced gradually.</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I consume Nutrimix if I have gluten intolerance?</strong>
A: Absolutely. Ragi, jowar, and green gram are naturally gluten‑free; the product contains no wheat, barley, or rye.</p>
<p><strong>Q: How does sprouting affect nutrient levels?</strong>
A: Sprouting activates enzymes that increase vitamin C, B‑vitamins, and mineral bioavailability while lowering phytate, which can inhibit iron and zinc absorption.</p>
<p><strong>Q: What is the shelf life of Nutrimix?</strong>
A: When stored in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, Nutrimix remains fresh for 6 months. Refrigeration extends freshness to a year.</p>
